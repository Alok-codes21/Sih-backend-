📦 How to Hand Off to Frontend Team / Remove Test UI

2
Clean up 2 lines in app.js
Remove lines 52-54 in app.js: 
app.use('/test-dashboard', express.static('public/test-dashboard'));
app.get('/', (req, res) => res.redirect('/test-dashboard/'));
3
Connect Your Production Frontend
Point your frontend (React, Next.js, Vue, Mobile App, etc.) to http://localhost:3000/api/v1/.
Set CORS_ORIGIN=http://localhost:5173 (or your frontend's port) in your .env file.
